About Insightara

We don't just report the numbers. We build what runs on them.

Insightara is a data and AI practice for businesses that want more than a dashboard nobody opens. We clean the mess, engineer the pipelines, train the models, and ship the AI that actually does the work — so every decision you make is backed by something real, not a gut feeling.

What we do

Four ways we work with data

01

Data Analytics

Cleaning, exploring, and visualizing data — dashboards and reports in Power BI, Excel, and Python that people actually use.

02

Data Engineering

Building reliable pipelines and data infrastructure with SQL and Python so your data is clean, structured, and ready to use.

03

Machine Learning

Regression, classification, and forecasting models built with scikit-learn — trained, tested, and tuned on your real data.

04

AI Engineering

Turning models into working products — APIs, RAG systems, and AI-powered features shipped into real applications.
